# What We Do

The Far West Security Patrol is a volunteer-based Citizens Radio Patrol group operating in Detroit's Far West neighborhood. These patrols serve as the "eyes and ears" for the Detroit Police Department (DPD), enhancing community safety by monitoring and reporting suspicious activities. Volunteers typically patrol in pairs for a few hours several times a month, fostering a sense of security among residents.
