# What We Do

The Far West Security Patrol is a volunteer-based Citizens Radio Patrol group operating in Detroit's Far West neighborhood.

We serve as the "eyes and ears" for the Detroit Police Department (DPD), enhancing community safety by monitoring and reporting suspicious activities.

Volunteers can patrol for as little as one hour per month or several hours multiple times per month.
