The Far West Detroit neighborhood that the patrol serves is home to the [Far West Detroit Civic Association (FWDCA)](https://www.farwestdetroit.org), a city-recognized neighborhood association.  We work closely with the FWDCA and many patrollers are involved with both organizations.

To find out more about the FWDCA and how you can get involved, please visit www.farwestdetroit.org.
